cURL converter

Paste a curl command (for example from your browser's “Copy as cURL”) and get the same request as code. Runs in your browser; nothing is sent.

    Supported options

    -X -H -d --data-raw --data-binary --data-urlencode --json -F --form-string -u -b -A -e -L -k --compressed -G -I -m --url --url-query --oauth2-bearer. Output-only flags such as -s -v -o -i are accepted and have no effect on the code; anything else is listed as a warning. Quoting follows the shell: '…', "…", $'…', backslashes and line continuations (\, ^, `). Files named with @file become placeholders, since the page can't read your disk.
