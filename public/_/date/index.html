How it counts

Years and months are added first and the day is clamped to the month's end, so 31 January + 1 month is 28 February (29 in a leap year). Then weeks and days move the calendar date, keeping the clock time. Hours are real elapsed time, so in local time 24 hours across a daylight-saving change can land an hour off.
