What these mean

Sample variance and standard deviation divide by n − 1 (use them when the numbers are a sample of a bigger group); population versions divide by n. Outliers are values more than 1.5 × IQR below Q1 or above Q3. Skewness is the adjusted Fisher-Pearson coefficient and kurtosis is excess kurtosis, matching Excel's SKEW and KURT. Percentiles use linear interpolation (the exclusive method when that is selected).
