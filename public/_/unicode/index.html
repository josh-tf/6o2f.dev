What the buttons do

NFC composes (e + ́ becomes é), NFD decomposes, the K forms also fold compatibility characters (ﬁ becomes fi, ² becomes 2). Strip invisibles removes zero-width, bidi and other format/control characters (keeping joiners inside emoji) and turns unusual spaces into plain spaces; lookalike letters are only flagged.
