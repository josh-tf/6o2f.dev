What it understands
  • ISO 8601: P1Y2M3DT4H5M6S, PT90M, P2W, PT0.5S, a leading - for negative.
  • Clock: 1:30:15 (h:mm:ss), 90:00 (m:ss), 1:02:03:04 (d:hh:mm:ss), fractions like 0:00:01.25.
  • Phrases: 2h30m, 90 minutes, 1.5 days, a week and 3 days, an hour and a half, half a day, 5 minutes ago.
  • Units: ms, s/sec, m/min, h/hr, d/day, w/wk, fortnight, mo/month, y/yr. Number words one to twelve, twenty, thirty and so on.
