Echo endpoint and parameters

/echo/<anything> answers any method with JSON describing what it received: method, path, query, headers (minus cookies and authorization) and the first 64 KB of the body.




/s/<status> takes delay (ms, max 10000), fail (0 to 1), failcode (default 500), retry (sent as Retry-After on 429 and 503 only), body (max 2048 bytes) and type (json or text). 204, 205 and 304 never have a body.
