    Where do I get a bookmarks file?

    Chrome and Edge: Bookmarks manager, the ⋮ menu, Export bookmarks. Firefox: Library (Ctrl+Shift+O), Import and Backup, Export Bookmarks to HTML. Safari: File, Export, Bookmarks.
