Privacy and limits

Details

Requests go to a small, fast open model (Llama 3.1 8B) on Cloudflare Workers AI. We do not keep your text; we only count requests. Up to 8,000 characters in, about 750 words out, and 100 requests per person per day (resets at 00:00 UTC).

AI output can be wrong or miss nuance. Check facts, names, numbers and code before you use them. Translation is strongest for English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Hindi and Thai.
