Track syntax

Sizes are separated by spaces: 1fr (a share of the free space), 200px, 25%, auto, minmax(120px, 1fr), repeat(3, 1fr). repeat(auto-fit, minmax(160px, 1fr)) fits as many columns as the width allows (auto-fill keeps empty columns, auto-fit collapses them).
